# USPTO TRADEMARK CLEARANCE & SEARCH STRATEGY
**Marks:** AEGIS-MONAD & PI-RAG ENGINE
**Classes:** Class 042 & Class 009

## 1. AEGIS-MONAD Clearance Strategy
* Queries: AEGIS MONAD, AEGIS-MONAD, (EGIS OR AEGIS) AND MONAD
* Risk Profile: Moderate. Compound connection with MONAD creates a distinctive mark.

## 2. PI-RAG ENGINE Clearance Strategy
* Queries: PI RAG, PIRAG, PI-RAG, PY RAG
* Risk Profile: Low/Moderate. Expect a standard disclaimer requirement for RAG ENGINE.
